# Open Source & Image Credits

Impulse is committed to ethical open-source attribution and licensing compliance.

## Third-Party Data & Image Licenses

1. **Wikimedia Commons & Wikidata**
   - **Content**: Cuisines, dish metadata, and food photography.
   - **Licenses**: The synchronizer accepts only Public Domain, CC0, CC BY, and CC BY-SA images and stores the exact license URL and creator attribution.
   - **Attributions**: Retained in database records and viewable directly under item details and the in-app Credits screen.
   - **Wikidata**: CC0 1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication.

2. **Open Food Facts**
   - **Content**: Packaged grocery names, categories, images, country tags, and barcode identifiers.
   - **License**: Open Database License (ODbL) v1.0 / Database Contents License (DbCL) v1.0.
   - **Images**: Product images are generally CC BY-SA 3.0; per-record attribution and source links are retained.
   - **Source**: [Open Food Facts](https://world.openfoodfacts.org/) database contributors.

3. **Open Icecat**
   - **Content**: Consumer electronics, computer components, appliances.
   - **License**: Open Icecat Open Content License (Free Catalog).
   - **Source**: [Icecat](https://icecat.biz/).

4. **Frankfurter Exchange Rates API**
   - **Content**: Foreign exchange reference rates.
   - **Source**: European Central Bank (ECB) data via Frankfurter open API.

5. **Photographs & Media**
   - Sample preview assets and fictional product placeholders courtesy of Unsplash photography under the Unsplash License (free for commercial and non-commercial use).

6. **MaxMind GeoLite2 Country**
   - **Content**: Local IP-to-country lookup data downloaded by the operator; no database is committed to this repository.
   - **License**: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International, subject to MaxMind's GeoLite2 terms and update requirements.
   - **Source**: [MaxMind GeoLite2](https://dev.maxmind.com/geoip/geolite2-free-geolocation-data/).
