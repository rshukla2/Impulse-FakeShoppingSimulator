Impulse

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 17, 2026

Impulse does not create user accounts and does not collect or store personal information in a user database. Impulse does not sell, rent, or use personal information for advertising. The app is a shopping simulator and does not process purchases, payments, or deliveries.

About Impulse

Impulse: Fake Shopping is a shopping simulator developed by Rishi Shukla. Users can browse catalogs, add items to a local cart, and place simulated orders. Nothing is purchased, no payment is made, and nothing is delivered.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the following information only in local application storage on your device:

  • The display name you choose to enter.
  • Your cart, simulated order history, and savings statistics.
  • Your onboarding status and the last country, currency, and exchange-rate response used by the app.

This local information is not sent to Impulse or stored in an Impulse user database. It remains on your device until you clear the app's storage, clear the site's local data, or uninstall the app.

No accounts, payments, or addresses

Impulse does not require or provide user accounts or authentication. The app does not request or provide fields for passwords, email addresses, phone numbers, delivery addresses, card numbers, bank information, or payment details. Impulse does not integrate with a payment processor.

Country and currency selection

When the app contacts the Impulse catalog service, the service temporarily processes the connection's IP address to infer a country and select an appropriate currency. This lookup does not use GPS. The IP address is not retained in the Impulse database or application logs and is not associated with a user profile.

Catalog requests and images

Search terms, filters, and catalog requests are processed only to return requested results. Impulse does not retain them or associate them with an identity. Catalog images may be loaded from providers or their content-delivery networks, including Open Food Facts, Open Icecat, Wikimedia Commons, and Unsplash. Those services may receive standard connection information when an image is requested and operate under their own privacy policies.

No advertising or tracking

Impulse does not include advertising, behavioral tracking, or analytics SDKs. Impulse does not sell or share personal information for advertising or marketing.

Device permissions

The Android app uses internet access to retrieve catalog information, images, country and currency information, and exchange rates. It does not request access to GPS location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or shared device storage.

Security, retention, and deletion

Connections to the Impulse backend use HTTPS. Because Impulse does not create accounts or store personal information on its servers, there is no server-side personal profile to retain or delete. You can delete locally stored information by clearing the app's storage, clearing the website's local data, or uninstalling the app.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy and the relevant app-store disclosures will be updated before those changes are released. The effective date at the top of this page identifies the latest version.
